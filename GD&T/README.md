# Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing (GD&T) — Complete Course

Welcome to the **GD&T Complete Course**, a structured learning series designed to introduce the principles of Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing and help learners understand engineering drawings, dimensional requirements, and geometric controls.

This course is part of my Mechanical Engineering portfolio and focuses on presenting technical concepts through visual explanations and practical engineering examples.

## 🎯 Course Objectives

- Understand the fundamentals of GD&T and its importance in mechanical engineering.
- Learn how to interpret engineering drawings and tolerances.
- Understand geometric symbols, datums, and feature control frames.
- Develop practical skills for reading and interpreting technical drawings.
